# BenchFleet

**Control real Android phones, yours or in the cloud, for people and AI agents.**

BenchFleet is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that runs Android phones from your computer: your own phones on USB, and cloud phones rented by the minute.

- **AI agents on real phones.** Claude Code, Codex and other AI clients use the phones you choose over MCP, with approvals for sensitive steps and a hash-chained audit log.
- **App testing on real phones.** Run recorded workflows and Maestro, Espresso and Appium suites on your own phones with a video of every run, or your instrumentation tests on a cloud phone from GitHub Actions.
- **Running a fleet.** See every phone live, take control of any of them, and automate the work with workflows and schedules.
- **Cloud phones.** A fresh Android phone (Android 11 to 17) on BenchFleet's servers, ready in about a minute, that joins your fleet next to your own phones and is deleted when it stops.

The app runs on your computer: your own phones, screens and data stay with you, and there is no telemetry.

**Website:** [benchfleet.com](https://benchfleet.com) · **Download:** [Windows, macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux](https://benchfleet.com/download) · **Cloud phones:** [benchfleet.com/cloud-phones](https://benchfleet.com/cloud-phones) · **Support:** support@benchfleet.com

## Open-source tools

- [benchfleet-mcp](https://github.com/benchfleet/benchfleet-mcp): the MCP connector for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other AI clients.
- [benchfleet-appium](https://github.com/benchfleet/benchfleet-appium): run your existing Appium tests on the phones connected to a BenchFleet Hub.
- [cloud-phone](https://github.com/benchfleet/cloud-phone): a GitHub Action that runs your Android tests on a BenchFleet cloud phone.
- [examples](https://github.com/benchfleet/examples): ready-to-copy workflows, Maestro flows, CI and MCP configurations.
