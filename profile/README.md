# BenchFleet

**A self-hosted control hub for real Android phones, for people and AI agents.**

BenchFleet is a desktop app that runs your own Android phones from your computer:

- **AI agents on real phones.** Claude Code, Codex and other AI clients use the phones you choose over MCP, with approvals for sensitive steps and a hash-chained audit log.
- **App testing on your own phones.** Run tests and workflows on real devices instead of renting a cloud device farm.
- **Running a fleet.** See every phone live, take control of any of them, and automate the work with workflows and schedules.

It runs on your computer: your phones, screens and data stay with you, and there is no telemetry.
